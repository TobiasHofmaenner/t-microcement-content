# Meet the founders

Three people are behind T-Microcement. Different fields, one material.

## Tobias Hofmänner

Physics lab technician, with a background in physics, engineering,
and product development. Tobias leads the formulation work: what's
in the system, why it's there, and how it has to behave in use.

## Martina Fink

Architect and interior designer, with an IT background. Martina
works the design and construction side: how the surfaces sit in a
room, how they meet the rest of the building, and how the system
has to perform in daily use.

## Conrad Kenway

Real estate, sales, and project development. Conrad reads the
market: what owners and developers ask about first, and what the
material has to deliver commercially.

---

Five working perspectives on one material: science, engineering,
architecture, design, and the property market. That mix is what
shaped the system.
