# The product

T-Microcement is a thin, seamless cementitious finish — 2 to 3 mm
total — applied directly over your existing walls, floors, bathrooms
and furniture. The result is a continuous modern surface, with no
joints, no grout, and no level changes.

## The 3-layer system

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Primer** | Ensures strong adhesion to whatever you're coating — concrete, plaster, tile, plywood. |
| **Microcement** | The design and texture layer. Trowelled by hand. This is where the look comes from. |
| **Sealer** | Final protection. Locks in waterproofing and stain resistance. |

## Where it works

- **Walls** — accent walls, full rooms, indoor or covered outdoor.
- **Floors** — residential and light commercial. 3 mm thin, no thresholds.
- **Bathrooms** — walls, floors and shower areas. Waterproof, joint-free.
- **Kitchens** — countertops, backsplashes, floors.
- **Furniture** — built-in cabinetry, vanities, custom pieces.

## Technical data

- **Thickness** — 2–3 mm total system
- **Coverage** — one 5 kg bucket covers ~2.5 m²
- **Drying** — walkable at 24 h (80%) · fully cured at 30 days (100%)
- **Hardness** — cures to a dense, hard, stone-like surface
- **UV** — mineral-based, won't yellow like resin finishes
- **Adhesion** — strong bonding with proper substrate prep, no delamination observed

## On durability — being straight with you

This is a custom-developed formulation. We have **not** put it through
formal laboratory testing, so we don't have certified performance
values to quote. We chose to be transparent about that rather than
borrow numbers from a generic spec sheet.

What we can tell you, from a year of using it in our own home and
field projects:

- **Abrasion** — handles daily foot traffic well in residential use, with the sealer doing its job.
- **Impact** — polymer-modified, so it has some give. Still a thin cementitious layer though — don't drop a hammer on it.
- **Water** — base material is dense, but the sealer is what makes it fully waterproof. Don't skip the sealer.
- **Biology** — no mould, no growth in indoor tropical conditions over the year we've tracked.
- **UV** — no yellowing, no fading.
- **Adhesion** — strong, no delamination, *as long as the substrate is properly prepared*.

The honest disclaimer: because it's a thin-layer system, the substrate
underneath matters as much as the coating itself. A weak or moving
surface will telegraph through. Site prep isn't optional.

We describe it as a **durable, high-quality microcement system for
residential applications** with real-world performance we can show
you. It is **not a certified industrial product**, so for commercial
or high-stress applications, evaluate per project — and we'll tell
you honestly whether your case is one we'd take on.
