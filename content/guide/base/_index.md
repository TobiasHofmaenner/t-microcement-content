# T-Microcement base

The look layer. Two thin trowelled coats over the primer.

## 1. Mix the base

<div class="video-short">
  <iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/9hsS0EGZcMo" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen title="T-Microcement mixing base powder and liquid"></iframe>

The bucket holds a powder and a liquid component. Add clean site water
to bring the mix to trowel consistency.

## 2. Apply the base

<div class="video-short">
  <iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/2Siejlp2ob8" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen title="T-Microcement applying base"></iframe>

Two thin passes. The look comes from the trowel technique.

**Products:** TM-BW-01 / TM-BW-02 (wall and ceiling, 10 kg),
TM-BF-01 / TM-BF-02 (floor, 15 kg).
**Coverage:** ~6 m² per bucket.

For which mix to pick, see
[Which base mix do I need?](/guide/#which-base-mix-do-i-need) on the guide.
